Task 1. Set the squeezing to 0 dB, then back to 5 dB. Predict: do the true displacements change? Do the samples move closer to them? What happens to the RMS error?

The displacements stay the same at every setting: squeezing changes only the noise $\eta$. At 5 dB the noise variance per part is 0.16 instead of 0.5, so the samples lie closer to the true displacements in the left plot. The RMS error falls from 0.21 at 0 dB to 0.04 at 5 dB, and the baseline needs about 26 times as many samples.
