Goal 3: at 5 dB and $b=1.5$, you double $N$. How does the RMS error change? And if you go from 5 dB to 0 dB at the same $N$?

Doubling $N$ divides the RMS error by $\sqrt2$, to about 0.71 of its value. Going to 0 dB multiplies it by about $\sqrt{26}\approx5$, because the baseline needs about 26 times as many samples for the same error.
