#### The scheme with a memory as a circuit

The circuit shows one use with $\alpha=0.9+0.1i$ and 5 dB of squeezing.
Wire 0 is the probe and wire 1 is the memory. From left to right:

- S: two-mode squeezing with $r=5\ln(10)/20=0.58$ prepares the two-mode squeezed vacuum.
- D: the displacement of the probe, with $\lvert\alpha\rvert=0.91$ and $\arg\alpha=0.11$.
- BS: a 50:50 beamsplitter, with angle $\pi/4=0.79$. It maps the two combinations onto single modes:
  after it, $X$ of wire 0 equals $(X_\text{p}-X_\text{m})/\sqrt2$, and $P$ of wire 1 equals $(P_\text{p}+P_\text{m})/\sqrt2$.
- Two homodyne detectors measure $X$ on wire 0 and $P$ on wire 1.
  Together with the beamsplitter, they form the Bell measurement.
