Task 3. Return to 2,000 samples and raise $b$ from 1.5 to 2. Predict: which estimate gets worse faster, the one at 0 dB or the one at 5 dB?

The one at 0 dB. The RMS error grows about as $e^{\nu b^2}$, with $\nu=1$ at 0 dB and 0.32 at 5 dB. At $b=2$ that factor is 55 at 0 dB and 3.5 at 5 dB. The RMS errors become 1.22 and 0.077, and the sample ratio rises to about 250.
