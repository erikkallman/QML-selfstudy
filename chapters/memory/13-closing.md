### What this case shows about QML

In the predictor and generator chapters, the quantum resource is a circuit model.
Here the model is a classical formula, the estimator, and the quantum resource is the memory used to collect the data.
With one mode, the memory reduces the sample complexity by a constant factor.
With $n$ modes, the factor grows exponentially with $n$, and for the study's hypothesis test the gap is proven.
The memory helps only because the queries arrive after the samples.
That makes this a learning task, not the estimation of one known parameter.
