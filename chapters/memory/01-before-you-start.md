### Before you start

This chapter follows one study of learning from quantum data, then simulates its central idea in code.

#### What you will be able to do

1. Explain how a quantum memory lowers the noise of each sample.
2. Explain how the estimator removes the bias of the noise, and predict its error.
3. Say what the study proved, measured, and estimated.

#### What you need

Complex numbers, the Fourier transform, mean and variance, and the Models chapter.
From quantum mechanics: states, observables, commutators, and measurement.

#### How to use the chapter

The Tutorial tab takes about 20 minutes. Try each question before you open its answer.
The panel below defines every term.
The Study tab describes the experiment (10 minutes, optional).
The Lab tab holds four experiments (15 minutes).
