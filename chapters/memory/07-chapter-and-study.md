A classical computer simulates one Gaussian mode exactly, in well under a second.
With one mode, squeezing reduces the sample complexity by a constant factor: about 26 at 5 dB and $b=1.5$.
Step 4 shows how the factor grows with the number of modes.
This chapter therefore shows the noise reduction per sample.

The Lab sets the effective squeezing: the squeezing that remains after optical losses, which sets the noise of the samples.
