Samples that record only the $X$ quadrature of each output: which queries can they answer, and why?

Only queries $\beta=ib$ on the imaginary axis. The wave $e^{\alpha^*\beta-\beta^*\alpha}=e^{2i(b_2a_1-b_1a_2)}$ depends on $a_2=\operatorname{Im}\alpha$ unless $b_1=\operatorname{Re}\beta=0$. Samples of $X$ carry no information about $\operatorname{Im}\alpha$, so they cannot estimate the mean of a wave that depends on it.
