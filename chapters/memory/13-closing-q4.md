Goal 4: why does Task 1 alone prove no advantage, while Task 2 does?

Task 1 compares the memory scheme with an extrapolated baseline. The paper's lower bound covers learning every device in a broad class, and a scheme that knows the family of Task 1 in advance could need fewer samples. Task 2 hides the family and reveals the query only after the measurements. For that test the authors prove a bound that holds for every scheme without a memory, and the measured success probability exceeds it.
