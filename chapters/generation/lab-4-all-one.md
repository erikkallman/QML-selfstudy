Task 4. Choose the input "All one". Predict the distribution of the 64 bitstrings, and the TVD between the target and the fitted circuit.

Every bitstring has probability $1/64$, and the TVD is 0. Every qubit starts in $\lvert0\rangle$, so every CZ gate acts as the identity and $R_Z$ adds only a phase factor. The final Hadamard then gives each bit probability $1/2$, whatever the angles.
