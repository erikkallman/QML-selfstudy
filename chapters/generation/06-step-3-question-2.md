Where does $\sin^2\beta$ come from?

Start in $\lvert0\rangle$. The Hadamard gives $(\lvert0\rangle+\lvert1\rangle)/\sqrt2$. $R_Z(2\beta)$ multiplies the two components by $e^{-i\beta}$ and $e^{i\beta}$. The final Hadamard gives outcome 1 the amplitude $(e^{-i\beta}-e^{i\beta})/2=-i\sin\beta$. Its squared modulus is $\sin^2\beta$.
