Task 3. Choose qubit 1 and the input "Isolate selected qubit". Predict the probability that qubit 1's output bit is 1, and whether it depends on the other qubits.

It is $\sin^2\hat\beta_1=0.253$ at 12,000 training pairs, close to the target's $\sin^2(0.51)=0.238$, and it does not depend on the other qubits. The neighbors of qubit 1 start in $\lvert0\rangle$, so every CZ gate on qubit 1 acts as the identity.
