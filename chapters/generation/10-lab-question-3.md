What changes when you isolate the selected qubit?

Its neighbors remain in $\lvert0\rangle$ up to a phase, so their CZ gates act as the identity on it. Its output bit is then 1 with probability $\sin^2\hat\beta_j$. The input All one instead prepares every qubit in $\lvert0\rangle$, which gives all 64 bitstrings probability $1/64$.
