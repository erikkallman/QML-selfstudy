### What this case shows about QML

When the circuit's graph is known, single-bit means determine its angles, although its output bits are correlated.
The chapter does not show a quantum advantage: six qubits are easy to simulate, and it compares with no classical generative model.
In research case 1, the model was a classical formula and the quantum resource acted in data collection.
Here the quantum resource is the model itself: the circuit produces the samples.
